🔥 Firebase Setup - 2 Minutes!

⏱️ This setup takes less than 2 minutes and is completely FREE!
🎉 Good News: Firebase is 100% free for authentication - no credit card required!
1

Create Firebase Project

  • Click the button below to open Firebase Console
  • Click "Add project" → Enter project name: "DEVFORGE"
  • Click "Continue" → Disable Google Analytics (optional)
  • Click "Create project" → Wait for setup
🚀 Open Firebase Console
2

Enable Google Authentication

  • Click "Authentication" in the left menu
  • Click "Get started" button
  • Under "Sign-in method" → Click "Google"
  • Toggle "Enable" → Select your email for testing
  • Click "Save"
🔐 Enable Google Auth
3

Get Your Configuration

  • Click the gear icon ⚙️ next to "Project Overview"
  • Select "Project settings"
  • Scroll down to "Your apps" section
  • Click the web app icon ()
  • Copy the firebaseConfig object
⚙️ Project Settings
4

Update Your Configuration

Replace the content in src/config/firebase.js with your config:

// Replace this entire section: const firebaseConfig = { apiKey: "your-api-key-here", authDomain: "your-project.firebaseapp.com", projectId: "your-project-id", storageBucket: "your-project.appspot.com", messagingSenderId: "123456789", appId: "your-app-id-here" };
5

Test Your Setup!

  • Save the firebase.js file
  • Restart your dev server: npm run dev
  • Go to http://localhost:4000
  • Click "Login" → "Google"
  • Sign in with your Google account
🧪 Test Your App
🎯 That's It! Your Gmail login will now work perfectly with Firebase - completely free and secure!
